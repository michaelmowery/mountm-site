Noodlit started on a foggy shower door: you write in the steam, and the fog quietly takes it back. Scribble, sketch or type whatever is rattling around, then let it go, so the next idea always has room.

There are six modes and 53 scenes, each defined by how strokes look, how they decay and how they clear. Fade scenes like Breath on glass disappear on their own. Release scenes like Paper boat carry the drawing off when you let go. Become, Reveal, Stir and Echo scenes turn your lines into fireflies, swirl them into latte art, or play them back as a tune.
