Classic Klondike with the Telluride Seasons cards. Draw one or three, drag and drop or tap to auto-move, undo, hints and auto-finish. Numbered deals can be shared and replayed, and games in progress and win stats are saved in the browser. It's a static site with no build step and a tested rules engine.
