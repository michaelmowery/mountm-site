Telluride.Buzz pulls what locals and visitors check every morning into one screen. It shows current weather and snow in the forecast, CDOT road conditions on CO-145, CO-62 and US-550, overnight snow and lifts and runs open at the ski resort, and upcoming events. A live feed below it collects updates from the resort, CDOT COtrip and San Miguel County, each linked back to its source.
